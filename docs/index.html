Getting started

Open Jira and choose Apps → Capacity Planner. The app reads Jira with your own permissions, so you only see the projects, sprints and people you can already see in Jira. It never changes your issues.

  1. Create a capacity template for your team.
  2. In Capacity tracking, choose a project, board and sprint, and apply the template.
  3. Add holidays or leave, adjust individual days, and save the plan.

Templates

A template lists the team and how much each person can take on:

  • Capacity per day — hours (or story points) a person works per day.
  • Focus — the share of the day spent on sprint work (0–1), e.g. 0.8 for meetings and support.
  • Allocation % — the share of the person's time given to this team. If someone works for two teams in the same fortnight, their allocations should add up to 100%.
  • Location — which location-specific holidays apply (for example India or USA).
  • Role — used for role reports.

Applying a template to a sprint copies it into that sprint's plan, so later template edits never change past plans. Use Re-apply to pick up changes.

Capacity tracking

The planning grid shows each person's capacity per day for the sprint. Weekends, holidays and leave are shaded; type a different number in any day to plan reduced or extra capacity, and clear it to go back to the default.

The summary shows total, allocated and remaining capacity, the balance and utilisation, and highlights people who are overloaded or under-utilised. Allocated work is the original estimate (or story points) of the sprint issues assigned to each person.

If someone's allocations across overlapping sprints add up to more than 100%, a warning names them so their time is not counted twice.

Holidays & leave

Add company holidays for everyone, or holidays for one location only. Add leave for a person with a date range. These apply automatically to every sprint plan, and each day is removed from capacity only once.

Forecast

In Capacity tracking, choose Analyse history to learn from the board's recently closed sprints:

  • Delivery range — how much work the team typically finishes per unit of capacity, applied to this sprint's capacity, with a verdict on the current commitment (comfortable, likely, stretch, unlikely).
  • Measured focus — hours each person logged on sprint issues during the sprint, compared with the hours they were available. Click Use to apply it to the plan, then save.
  • Estimate accuracy — time spent compared with original estimates on finished issues, and the likely actual effort of this sprint's work.

Only work finished during each sprint, by people in the template, is counted. Suggestions need a few sprints of history.

Reports

Compare up to six sprints by team, sprint, team member, role and resource utilisation (time logged against capacity, week by week). Choose Hours or Story points to match the sprint plans. Save a report as private or public, and export it to CSV.

Settings

Jira administrators can choose which issue types count, how remaining work is calculated, the overload and under-utilisation thresholds, the story point field, and whether only administrators may change templates.
